# Minimal mascot logo explorations v4

These marks deliberately reduce the supplied mascot to its most recognizable signals: a rounded visor, two eyes, and either an antenna or whiteboard corner.

They use exact solid design-system colors with no gradients, shadows, textures, or generated edge artifacts:

- Ink: `#0C0E17`
- Surface: `#FFFFFF`
- Brand violet: `#6051DC`

## Directions

1. **Visor signal** — the smallest and most distinctive mascot reduction.
2. **Orb visor** — a contained app-icon form with stronger mass.
3. **Board corner** — combines the tutor and whiteboard ideas without depicting the full character.

Each SVG is built on a `512 × 512` viewBox and can be exported losslessly at any required size.
